Zuora Product Sync

Status

All products and rate plans are synced with Zuora. Products must be synced to Zuora before customers can subscribe.

Note: This action creates real products in your Zuora tenant. Ensure your credentials are configured correctly.

How It Works

            

            

                
    
                    
  1. Create Products Locally - Add membership products and rate plans in the Products section
    2. 
                    
  2. Sync to Zuora - Click "Sync All Products to Zuora" to create them in Zuora
    3. 
                    
  3. Verify Sync - Check that all products show "Synced" status with Zuora IDs
    4. 
                    
  4. Ready for Subscriptions - Customers can now subscribe to membership plans
